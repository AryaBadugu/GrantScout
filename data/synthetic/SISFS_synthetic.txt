STARTUP INDIA SEED FUND SCHEME (SISFS) - SYNTHETIC SAMPLE
[SYNTHETIC DATA FOR TESTING ONLY - NOT A REAL DOCUMENT]

Scheme Name: Startup India Seed Fund Scheme (SISFS)
Issuing Body: Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT), Ministry of Commerce and Industry, Government of India
Funding Amount: Up to ₹50 lakh (equity-free grant up to ₹20 lakh for proof of concept; up to ₹50 lakh for prototype development and trials)
Eligible Stages: Ideation, Prototype, Early-stage
Sectors: Any sector (preference to deeptech, agritech, healthtech, cleantech, edtech, social impact)
Location: Pan-India
Application Link: https://seedfund.startupindia.gov.in/

Eligibility Criteria:
1. Startup must be recognized by DPIIT under Startup India
2. Startup should be incorporated not more than 2 years ago at time of application
3. Startup must have a business idea to develop a product or service with market fit, viable commercialization, and scalability
4. Startup must not have received more than ₹10 lakh in monetary support from Central or State Government schemes
5. Promoters of the startup must hold at least 51% shareholding in the startup
6. The startup must not have been funded by a Venture Capital firm, angel network, private equity firm, or incubator

Required Documents:
1. DPIIT recognition certificate
2. Certificate of incorporation
3. Detailed project/business plan
4. Pitch deck (10-15 slides)
5. Financial projections (3-year)
6. Aadhar and PAN of founders
7. Bank account details of the startup

Deadline: Rolling basis – applications accepted throughout the year. Check official portal for cohort deadlines.
Application Process: Apply through registered incubators under SISFS. List of approved incubators available on the official website.
