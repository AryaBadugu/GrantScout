Scheme Name: MSME Technology Upgradation Support Scheme
Issuing Body: Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSME), Government of India
Funding Amount: Up to ₹1 crore (50% subsidy on technology upgradation investment, max ₹1 crore per unit)
Eligible Stages: Early-stage, Growth, Scaling
Sectors: Manufacturing, Deeptech, Any (preference to traditional industries adopting new tech)
Location: Pan-India (additional preference to aspirational districts)
Application Link: https://msme.gov.in/

Eligibility Criteria:
1. Must be a registered MSME (Udyam Registration mandatory)
2. Annual turnover not exceeding ₹250 crore
3. Must not have availed similar benefit under any Central Government scheme in last 3 years
4. Technology to be adopted must result in at least 20% energy or productivity improvement
5. Startup/MSME must be in operation for at least 1 year

Required Documents:
1. Udyam Registration Certificate
2. Detailed Project Report (DPR)
3. Last 2 years audited financial statements
4. Bank solvency certificate
5. Quotations from technology providers
6. PAN and GST registration
7. Board resolution (for companies)

Deadline: Applications accepted on rolling basis. Subject to annual budget availability.
