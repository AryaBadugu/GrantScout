Scheme Name: NIDHI-PRAYAS (Promoting and Accelerating Young and Aspiring Innovators & Startups)
Issuing Body: Department of Science and Technology (DST), National Science and Technology Entrepreneurship Development Board (NSTEDB)
Funding Amount: Up to ₹10 lakh per innovator/team (grant for prototype development)
Eligible Stages: Ideation, Prototype
Sectors: Technology-led innovations – deeptech, cleantech, agritech, healthtech, manufacturing, any science and technology domain
Location: Pan-India (through DST-approved host institutions / incubators)
Application Link: https://www.dst.gov.in/nidhi

Eligibility Criteria:
1. Individual innovators, students, or startup teams with a technology idea
2. Must not be an existing startup (pre-incorporation stage preferred)
3. Must be affiliated with or apply through a DST-NIDHI approved incubator/host institution
4. Technology must have clear commercial potential
5. Not more than 51% funding from other Central Government schemes for the same idea
6. Indian citizen(s) as primary applicant

Required Documents:
1. Detailed innovation/project proposal
2. Prototype plan and milestones
3. CV of team members
4. Letter of recommendation from host institution
5. IP assessment (if any existing IP)

Deadline: Announced per cohort by the host incubator. Typically 2-3 cohorts per year.
