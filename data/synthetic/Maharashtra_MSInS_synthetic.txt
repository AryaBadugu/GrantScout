MAHARASHTRA STATE INNOVATION SOCIETY - STARTUP GRANT - SYNTHETIC SAMPLE
[SYNTHETIC DATA FOR TESTING ONLY - NOT A REAL DOCUMENT]

Scheme Name: Maharashtra Startup Week / MSInS Innovation Grant
Issuing Body: Maharashtra State Innovation Society (MSInS), Department of Skill Development, Employment, Entrepreneurship and Innovation, Government of Maharashtra
Funding Amount: Up to ₹15 lakh per startup (non-dilutive grant)
Eligible Stages: Ideation, Prototype, Early-stage
Sectors: Any sector with preference to agritech, edtech, healthtech, cleantech, and digital solutions for rural Maharashtra
Location: Maharashtra only (startup must be registered in Maharashtra)
Application Link: https://msins.in/

Eligibility Criteria:
1. Startup must be registered in Maharashtra (Pvt Ltd, LLP, OPC, or Registered Partnership)
2. DPIIT recognition preferred but not mandatory
3. Startup age: not more than 5 years from date of incorporation
4. At least one founder must be a Maharashtra domicile (permanent resident)
5. Revenue not exceeding ₹25 crore in the previous financial year
6. Must not have received state grant from MSInS in the previous 2 years

Required Documents:
1. Maharashtra incorporation / registration proof
2. Aadhaar and PAN of lead founder (Maharashtra domicile proof)
3. Business plan and pitch deck
4. Product/prototype demo video (link)
5. Financial projections (2-year minimum)
6. GST registration (if applicable)

Deadline: Typically announced in Q3 of each fiscal year (October-December). Check MSInS website.
