SIDBI STARTUP MITRA - FUND OF FUNDS - SYNTHETIC SAMPLE
[SYNTHETIC DATA FOR TESTING ONLY - NOT A REAL DOCUMENT]

Scheme Name: SIDBI Fund of Funds for Startups (FFS)
Issuing Body: Small Industries Development Bank of India (SIDBI) / Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT)
Funding Amount: Up to ₹10 crore (via Alternative Investment Funds; SIDBI commits ₹5,000 crore corpus)
Eligible Stages: Early-stage, Growth, Scaling
Sectors: Any sector recognized under Startup India
Location: Pan-India
Application Link: https://www.sidbi.in/en/products-schemes/startup-ecosystem

Eligibility Criteria:
1. Startup must be DPIIT recognized
2. Must be incorporated in India (Private Limited, LLP, or Partnership)
3. No direct funding – investment routed via SEBI-registered Alternative Investment Funds (AIFs)
4. AIFs applying must demonstrate portfolio of Indian startups
5. Startup must not be in prohibited/restricted sectors (gambling, tobacco, real estate speculation)
6. Age of startup: Not older than 10 years from date of incorporation

Required Documents (for AIF route):
1. DPIIT recognition certificate
2. Certificate of incorporation
3. Term sheet from AIF
4. Cap table and shareholder agreement
5. Last 2 years financials (if available)
6. Business plan and financial projections

Deadline: Continuous basis through registered AIFs. No fixed annual deadline.
